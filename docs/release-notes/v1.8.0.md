A light theme, and a switch for it in the top bar.

## Light or dark

The app was dark only, which is the wrong answer in a bright room and the wrong answer for anyone who finds white-on-black hard to read.

There is a switch in the top bar now, next to Settings. It shows where it will take you rather than where you are — a sun on the dark theme, a moon on the light one.

Three things worth knowing:

- **It is remembered per machine**, so a laptop and a bright desktop can disagree. The choice is not part of your settings file; it belongs to the screen you are looking at.
- **A machine that has never chosen follows the system**, and keeps following it — so a PC or Mac that switches itself to light at sunset takes the app along. Press the button once and your choice wins from then on.
- **No flash on launch.** The theme is applied before the window draws anything, so the app does not open dark and turn white a moment later.

The dark theme is unchanged. Not "close to unchanged" — the work was done by giving every colour a name and letting the light theme point those names somewhere else, so the dark values are the same values they were.

## Both themes were checked, not eyeballed

Every piece of text in both themes was measured against what is actually behind it, at the contrast floor for its size. A few things only a check would have caught:

- The top bar's glass was a hardcoded near-black, and would have stayed dark over a white page.
- The duration and rank badges on a clip sit on a video frame, which is black in both themes. Following the theme would have turned them black-on-black.
- The quietest grey, which looked right beside the other light colours, was under the readable floor on white. Grey on white is harder to read than the same grey on black.

## Nothing else changed

No changes to how videos are downloaded, transcribed, ranked or rendered. The Windows and Mac builds are rebuilt from the same code.
